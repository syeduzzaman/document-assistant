# Repair estimate evidence (v1)

Fictional Nordwerk Auto training material. Applies to front bumper replacement estimates.

## Photos

- Take one wide photo of the vehicle's front before removing the bumper.
- Take close-up photos of the damaged bumper and any damaged mounting points.

## Documents

- Attach the itemized estimate showing parts, labor, and paint.
- Attach the teardown findings to the revised estimate.
