# Nordwerk Auto — Support playbook

This is fictional demonstration data for Document Assistant.

## Priority P1
A P1 incident is a complete service outage affecting all workshops.
For a P1 incident, the first response target is 15 minutes.
The incident commander posts a status update every 30 minutes until service is restored.

## Priority P2
A P2 incident affects a major feature, but a workaround exists.
For a P2 incident, the first response target is four business hours.

## Escalation
Escalate unresolved P1 incidents to the engineering lead after 30 minutes.
Never include customer passwords or access tokens in a support ticket.
Support coverage runs from 08:00 to 18:00 Europe/Berlin, Monday through Friday.
