# Nordwerk Auto — Employee handbook

This is fictional demonstration data for Document Assistant.

## Annual leave
Full-time employees receive 30 days of paid annual leave per calendar year.
Submit annual leave requests through the staff portal at least two weeks before the first day.
A team lead must approve the request before travel is booked.

## Remote work
Employees may work remotely up to three days per week with their team lead's approval.
Customer reception and workshop appointments must always have on-site coverage.
Core working hours are 10:00 to 15:00 on weekdays.

## Learning
Each employee has an annual learning budget of EUR 800.
The learning budget can cover technical courses, books and certification exams with manager approval.
Unused learning budget expires at the end of the calendar year.
